# HANUMAN JI · LANKA DAHAN
Ravan ne Hanuman ji ki poonch mein aag lagwa di.

Rakshas hans rahe the.

Par Hanuman ji muskuraye.

Ek chhalaang...

aur ghar ghar mein aag!

Sone ki Lanka jal uthi.

Ravan ka ghamand bhi jal gaya.

Hanuman ji ne samudra mein poonch bujhayi.

Aur Ram naam goonj utha.

Jai Shri Ram!
